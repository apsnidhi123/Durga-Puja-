MAHA NAVRATRI & DASHAMI TITHI TIMELINE

Auspicious timings and rituals observed across Bihar Sharif

Maha Shasthi

Maa Bodhon & Adhivash

Evening Bilva Nimantran

Awakening of the Goddess under the sacred Bel tree followed by installation of sanctified Kalash and evening Aarti.

Maha Saptami

Nabapatrika Snan

Brahma Muhurat (Dawn)

The sacred bath of 'Kola Bou' (nine holy plant forms of Durga) and Pran Pratishtha across all grand pandals.

Maha Ashtami

Kumari Puja & Sandhi Puja

Sandhi Kaal: 108 Deepam

The climax worship at the cusp of Ashtami and Navami where Maa Chamunda vanquished Chanda and Munda.

Maha Navami

Maha Havan & Dandiya

Purna Ahuti • Night Dandiya

Grand cosmic sacrifice and Vedic Ahuti followed by energetic Dhunuchi dance contests and Dandiya beats.

Vijayadashami

Sindoor Khela & Visarjan

Shobha Yatra (Afternoon)

Married women perform Devi Boron and joyful Sindoor Khela before the regal immersion procession (Visarjan).
