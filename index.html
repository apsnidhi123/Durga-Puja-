MAHA NAVRATRI & DASHAMI TITHI TIMELINE

Auspicious timings and sacred rituals observed across Bihar Sharif

Maha Shasthi

Maa Bodhon & Adhivash

Evening Bilva Tree Invocation

Awakening of the Goddess under the sacred Bel tree followed by installation of sanctified Kalash and evening Aarti.

Maha Saptami

Nabapatrika Snan

Brahma Muhurat (Dawn Bath)

The sacred bath of 'Kola Bou' (nine holy plant forms of Durga) and Pran Pratishtha across all grand pandals.

Maha Ashtami

Kumari Puja & Sandhi Puja

Sandhi Kaal: 108 Sacred Lamps

The climax worship at the cusp of Ashtami and Navami where Maa Chamunda vanquished the demons Chanda and Munda.

Maha Navami

Maha Havan & Dandiya

Purna Ahuti • Night Dandiya

Grand cosmic sacrifice and Vedic Ahuti followed by energetic Dhunuchi dance contests and rhythmic Dandiya beats.

Vijayadashami

Sindoor Khela & Visarjan

Immersion Procession (Afternoon)

Devoted women perform Devi Boron and joyful Sindoor Khela before the regal immersion procession (Visarjan).
