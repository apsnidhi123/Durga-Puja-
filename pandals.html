Why Bihar Sharif's Pandals Are Renowned Across Bihar

During Navratri, the streets of Bihar Sharif transform into a massive cultural confluence. Master sculptors from Kumartuli (Kolkata) and local Nalanda clay artisans spend months shaping sacred soil from the holy Ganges. Pandals here are renowned for architectural replicas (such as the Amarnath Cave, Akshardham Temple, and historical Indian monuments), dazzling LED light gates, and deep communal harmony where thousands gather for all-night parikramas.

🗺️ Suggested Bihar Sharif Pandal Parikrama Route

Planning an evening walk with family? Follow this connected route from North-East entry to West Bihar Sharif to cover all 7 iconic pandals smoothly. Click any stop to open Google Maps!

STOP 1 • 6:00 PM

Hospital Mor (Badi Pahari)

Start at the grand highway light gates & 20ft Daaker Saaj idol.

STOP 2 • 7:00 PM

Bhaisasur Chauraha

Walk down Ranchi Road to witness the 150-yr heritage cave pandal.

STOP 3 • 8:00 PM

Amber Mor (Kachahri)

Experience Vedic temple replicas & evening Dhunuchi Aarti.

STOP 4 • 9:00 PM

Pulpar & Mohaddinegar

Cross the illuminated market bridge & see Parilok animatronics.

STOP 5 • 10:15 PM

Khandakpar & Ramchandrapur

Conclude with 1000-Diya Aarti & the grand Ramchandrapur Mela.
