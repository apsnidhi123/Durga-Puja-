Day-by-Day Rituals & Festivals

From the auspicious dawn of Mahalaya to Vijayadashami Sindoor Khela and Dandiya Raas nights.

Ashwin Amavasya • Day 0

Mahalaya & Chakshu Daan

Maa Durga is invoked from the heavens through chanting of Chandi Path. Master sculptors paint the sacred third eye on the idol (Chakshu Daan), breathing cosmic life into the clay.

Maha Shasthi • Day 6

Bodhan & Unveiling of the Pandal

The awakening of the Goddess under the sacred Bilva (Bel) tree. Pandals open their gates across Bihar Sharif as the Dhak drums thunder with community joy.

Maha Saptami • Day 7

Kola Bou Snan (Navapatrika)

Nine sacred botanical plants symbolizing Durga's nature forms are bathed at dawn, draped in a red-bordered yellow saree as 'Kola Bou', and welcomed beside Lord Ganesha.

🪀 Dandiya Night 1: Community grounds in Amber Chowk host vibrant Garba circles.
Maha Ashtami • Day 8

Kumari Puja & Sandhi Puja

Young girls are worshiped as living embodiments of the Goddess. During Sandhi Puja—the 48-minute juncture between Ashtami and Navami—108 blue lotuses and 108 clay diyas are lit.

🪀 Grand Dandiya Raas: Traditional chaniya cholis and clashing sticks late into the night.
Maha Navami • Day 9

Maha Homa & Dhunuchi Naach

The grand sacred fire sacrifice is solemnized. Devotees perform the exhilarating Dhunuchi Naach, balancing clay burners smoking with coconut husk and camphor.

Vijayadashami • Day 10

Sindoor Khela & Visarjan

Married women celebrate the sweet farewell of Maa Durga by smearing red vermilion (Sindoor Khela) before the sacred clay idols are immersed in holy waters.
